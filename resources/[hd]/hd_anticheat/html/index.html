World Reset

Clears stray or broken dynamic objects (knocked-over street furniture, dropped props, etc.) around every connected player. There's no single "reset the map" switch in the engine — this asks each player's own game to tidy up around wherever they actually are.

Sent to every connected player's chat, including your own, plus an on-screen banner.
